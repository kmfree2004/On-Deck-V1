On Deck

A concept website that helps young softball players see every future the sport can lead to, including playing pro with the AUSL. Choose a version below.

Version 3

Latest

November 18, 2026. Built from the project owner's own review of version 2.

  • Menus grouped into dropdowns: Explore, Real talk, Get recruited, and For coaches.
  • Pro spotlights redesigned around questions every player answers, with sample cards that can be browsed one question at a time.
  • The Guide is now read-only. Checklists moved into a new My plan section.
  • My plan: a profile, progress checklist, email builder, saved emails and school tracker. It saves in the visitor's own browser, with no account, and data can be downloaded and loaded back.

Earlier version

November 17, 2026. Built after the first round of feedback.

  • Recruiting guide expanded into four sections, including an email builder with templates and advice on choosing camps and showcases.

  • Coach toolkit tip on being patient with players who are not ready to explore their options.
